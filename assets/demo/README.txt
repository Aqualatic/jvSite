Original demo media

First Light, After Hours, and Slow Orbit are short instrumental synthesizer
sketches created for this project. All instruments are synthesized from
oscillators and generated noise by scripts/generate-demo.cjs. No third-party
recordings, melodies, samples, album covers, or external music services are used.

The matching covers are original SVG drawings.

These demo audio recordings and SVG illustrations may be used, copied,
modified, and redistributed for any purpose, including commercial use,
without payment or attribution. This permission applies only to the original
assets in assets/demo, not to the separate friends library.

To regenerate the audio: node scripts/generate-demo.cjs
