About Me

— the person behind the mask

This is a little corner of the internet where I share the sounds I've made, the covers I've recorded, and the tracks that mean something to me. It's not about algorithms or play counts it's just music, made by one person, for whoever wants to listen.

I started making music as a way to unwind, and it slowly became something I couldn't imagine life without. Everything here was recorded in my own space, with whatever gear I had at the time. You'll hear imperfections, and that's kind of the point it's honest.

If something here connects with you, drop a comment on the track. I read every one, and it genuinely means a lot. Thanks for listening.
